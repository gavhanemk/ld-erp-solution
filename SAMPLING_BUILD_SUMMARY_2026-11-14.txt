HOW THE RECORDS ARE ORGANISED
---------------------------
Request: The overall buyer enquiry or internal sample development request.
It holds the buyer, brand, brief, owner and required date.

Round: One attempt or milestone under that request. A round currently covers
one style, one colour and one or more sizes. Revisions remain under the same
request, preserving earlier specifications and buyer decisions.

Milestone: A descriptive name such as Development sample, Fit sample, Size set
or Pre-production sample. These are examples; the milestone is currently a
text field, not a fixed sequence of mandatory sample types.

Making step: An operation within a round, such as Cutting, Stitching,
Embroidery or Finishing. Making steps are different from sample milestones.

Current overall flow:
Request -> Technical specification and pattern references -> Release ->
Material issue -> Making steps -> QC -> Buyer submission -> Buyer decision ->
New round when required, or approved handover -> Production acceptance.

S01. SAMPLE REQUEST / BUYER ENQUIRY
---------------------------------
Built:
- Create a sample request with its own request number and initial draft round.
- Capture request title, brand, sampling owner and buyer brief/design references.
- Select a buyer, or use Internal development when there is no buyer.
- Optionally enter the buyer reference and required date.
- View requests in a searchable, paginated register.

Current rules and limits:
- Title, brand, owner and brief are required.
- The sampling owner is entered as a name, rather than assigned through a
  dedicated staff allocation or workload screen.
- A round uses an existing style from the selected brand and that style's
  existing colours and size run.
- Request details can be edited from Request & buyer brief, with a reason.
  Activity shows the previous and updated values. Buyer and brand lock after
  the first release; all request edits stop after cancellation or handover.
  Clear and save any selected draft style before changing its brand.

S02. TECHNICAL REVIEW & TECH PACK
-------------------------------
Built:
- Prepare the technical specification before release.
- Enter the milestone, style, colour and construction instructions.
- Enter tech-pack reference, pattern reference and optional technical notes.
- Define sample sizes and piece quantities.
- Define measurement points for each size, target values, tolerances and units
  in centimetres or inches.
- Plan materials and the sequence of making operations.

Current rules and limits:
- Save draft is available in every preparation section. Incomplete fields and
  unfinished rows can be saved to the local database and reopened later.
  Supplied master references and value limits are checked. Release still
  requires the complete specification and does not accept an incomplete draft.
- The UI keeps unsaved specification entries when switching workspace sections.
  This is temporary in-screen state, not automatic saving.
- Every selected size needs at least one measurement point.
- Release freezes that round's specification and creates its material and
  making records. Later technical changes require a new round.
- Documents are recorded as references; actual file/image uploads are not built.

S03. MATERIAL SOURCING / PREPARATION
----------------------------------
Built:
- Plan fabric, trims, accessories, packing materials and consumables using
  existing material masters.
- Choose the store, planned quantity and ownership for each material line.
- Support company-owned and buyer-owned material.
- After release, record material issue, use, waste and return, with a reason.
- Display planned, issued, used, wasted, returned and still-held quantities.
- Issue and return use the ERP's existing inventory movement system.
- Material values are shown only to users with accounts viewing permission.

Current rules and limits:
- Buyer-owned material requires a buyer and uses that buyer's stock.
- Material issue cannot exceed the released material plan.
- All planned material must be issued before making is recorded.
- New issues are allowed before the first making step is completed.
- Keep the planned material issued until the first making step is recorded;
  return surplus after that step, or reconcile it when cancelling the request.
- Recording use or waste accounts for material already issued; it does not
  deduct stock a second time.
- Used, wasted and returned quantities cannot exceed what the round holds.
- Material on earlier rounds can still be reconciled.
- Dedicated sourcing requests, supplier follow-up and sampling-linked purchase
  orders are not built. This stage currently plans and handles available stock.

S04. PATTERN DEVELOPMENT
-----------------------
Built:
- Record the pattern version or reference in the technical specification.
- Include construction, sizes, measurements and planned material quantities.
- Preserve the released reference when a revised pattern is used in a new round.

Current rules and limits:
- Pattern development is grouped with technical preparation in the interface.
- There is no separate pattern assignment, pattern approval or completion screen.
- Pattern-file upload, CAD integration, marker planning and automatic fabric
  consumption calculation are not built. Material requirements are entered.

S05. SAMPLE CUTTING
------------------
Built:
- Cutting is the first operation in the making route.
- Record completed pieces, rejected pieces, responsible person/unit, an optional
  work reference and notes about the work or rejected-piece disposition.

Current rules and limits:
- Completed plus rejected pieces must equal the pieces received by the step.
- Cutting receives the total planned sample quantity across the selected sizes.
- A step is recorded as a completed batch, not through partial progress entries.
- Detailed cutting markers, bundle tracking and piece-level custody are not built.

S06. SAMPLE STITCHING
--------------------
Built:
- Stitching is a required operation in the making route.
- Record the same completion, rejection, responsibility and reference details
  used for cutting.
- The next step receives the previous step's completed quantity.

Current rules and limits:
- Steps must be completed in their configured order.
- Future-step forms show their prerequisites instead of opening immediately.
- Detailed line allocation, operator time and stitching labour costs are not built.

S07. FINISHING & SPECIAL PROCESSES
--------------------------------
Built:
- The default making route is Cutting -> Stitching -> Finishing.
- Additional named steps can be added for washing, printing, embroidery,
  pressing or other work.
- The preparation form provides ordered rows to arrange the making sequence.
- Each operation records completed/rejected quantities, responsibility and notes.

Current rules and limits:
- Cutting stays first, Stitching must be included and step names must be unique.
- Finishing is in the default route; it is not enforced as a mandatory final step.
- Outside processing currently records a responsible unit and work references.
- Outside-unit challans, dispatch/receipt documents and service costing are not built.

S08. SAMPLE QC & MEASUREMENT
---------------------------
Built:
- QC has its own workspace section after making.
- Enter actual values against the released measurement targets.
- Display size, measurement name, unit and whether the value is within tolerance.
- Record pieces passing workmanship inspection and QC findings/disposition.
- Measurements outside tolerance keep the round from proceeding to submission.
- A failed inspection can be followed by a corrected inspection or a new round.

Current rules and limits:
- Every making operation must be complete before QC.
- QC cannot pass more pieces than the final making step produced.
- Every measurement point must have an actual value.
- For multi-size rounds, the pilot requires the complete size set through making,
  QC and submission. Partial rejection by size needs separate size rounds.
- Rework tasks within the same attempt and detailed defect categorisation are
  not built. QC findings are recorded in notes and the action history.

S09. BUYER SUBMISSION & APPROVAL
------------------------------
Built:
- Submit a QC-passed sample by recording pieces sent, recipient, courier or
  signed-handover reference, and submission/retained-sample notes.
- Record the buyer's decision: Approved, Changes required or Rejected.
- Record the buyer decision maker, evidence/feedback reference and any conditions.

Current rules and limits:
- Submission quantity cannot exceed the quantity passed by QC.
- A multi-size round must submit the complete inspected set.
- Approval cannot have unresolved conditions. Use Changes required while
  corrections remain outstanding.
- An authorised ERP user records the buyer's response manually.
- A buyer portal, automated email submission, courier integration and uploaded
  approval evidence are not built.

S10. REVISION OR NEXT SAMPLE STAGE
--------------------------------
Built:
- Start a new revision or milestone after buyer review, or after failed QC.
- Enter the reason and copy the previous specification into a new draft round.
- Change the milestone, pattern, measurements, materials or making route there.
- Switch between rounds to review earlier specifications and decisions.

Current rules and limits:
- Only the latest round can be changed, except material reconciliation on
  earlier rounds.
- The software does not force a fixed sequence such as Proto -> Fit -> Size set
  -> Pre-production. The actual milestone sequence still needs confirmation.
- Once a round has a prepared handover, new development needs another request.

S11. PRE-PRODUCTION APPROVAL & BULK HANDOVER
-----------------------------------------
Built:
- Prepare a handover after unconditional buyer approval.
- Select an existing approved production BOM (bill of materials) and an active
  production routing suitable for the style.
- Record handover notes and preserve the selected technical information with
  the sample specification and buyer decision.
- Record preparation and production acceptance, including the responsible
  person's name and date.

Current rules and limits:
- Material must be fully accounted for on every round before handover.
- Production acceptance requires production approval permission.
- Handover does not create or release a bulk manufacturing order.
- The milestone name itself does not enforce a special Pre-production approval
  gate. The required final milestone and authorised approvers need confirmation.
- Automatic production-order creation and sales-order linkage are not built.

U01. INTERFACE BUILT AND REFINED TODAY
------------------------------------
Sampling register:
- All requests.
- In progress: released rounds handling materials, making and QC.
- Ready for buyer / in review: QC-passed or submitted rounds.
- Approved & handover: buyer-approved rounds and their handovers.
- Search, request status, next-action text, CSV export and request opening.

Inside a request:
1. Request & buyer brief.
2. Technical review & pattern.
3. Material preparation.
4. Sample making, with visible Cutting/Stitching/Finishing or custom steps.
5. QC & measurements.
6. Buyer submission & review.
7. Production handover.
An additional Activity & request actions section holds history and cancellation.

The interface groups the eleven business stages above into these seven working
sections. Pattern development is within technical preparation; special processes
are within making; revision creation is within review or failed QC.

Form improvements:
- Four preparation sections: Style & construction; Sizes & measurements;
  Materials & making route; Review & release.
- A checklist of missing details before saving/releasing.
- A Next action prompt based on the selected round's current position.
- Separate completed, current, waiting and upcoming stage indications.
- Clearer material quantity summaries, measurement sizes/units and field hints.
- Unsaved specification protection when leaving a request or changing rounds.
- A standalone printable record covering the request and its rounds.

C01. RECORD PROTECTION, HISTORY AND CANCELLATION
----------------------------------------------
- Sampling has separate create, view, edit, approve and export permissions.
- Material actions also need inventory edit permission.
- Production acceptance also needs production approve permission.
- Conflicting saves from another window are refused rather than silently
  overwriting the newer record.
- Saved actions record who performed them and when.
- Released specifications and previous buyer decisions are preserved.
- Cancellation requires a reason and sample disposition, with material fully
  accounted for. A request with a prepared handover cannot be cancelled.
- Sample quantities are separate from bulk production and saleable garments.

N01. MAIN ITEMS NOT YET BUILT
----------------------------
- Actual tech-pack, design, pattern, photograph and approval-file uploads.
- Dedicated sourcing/purchasing links and supplier follow-up.
- Pattern/marker tools and automatic material-consumption calculations.

- Outside processing challans and dispatch/receipt workflows.
- Labour, processing, courier and complete sample costing/accounting.
- Partial making progress, detailed size-level rejection and rework tasks.

- A fixed milestone sequence and a mandatory final pre-production gate.
